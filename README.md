About Karpo Travel

Your Journey, Our Priority.

At Karpo Travel, we believe every journey deserves exceptional care. Founded by Jenson Daniel, a passionate traveler and adventurer, Karpo Travel draws inspiration from a family legacy spanning over 60 years in the global travel industry, with an international presence and millions of satisfied customers. Driven by his love for exploring new destinations, motorcycle adventures, hiking, diverse cultures, and authentic cuisines, Jenson began helping travelers with personalized travel solutions in 2023. What started as a passion for guiding others soon grew through trust, referrals, and increasing customer demand, leading to the official establishment of Karpo Travel in New York in 2026. Today, we combine generations of industry knowledge with a fresh, customer-focused approach to delivering seamless, reliable, and memorable travel experiences.
